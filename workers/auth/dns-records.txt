========================================
 DNS RECORDS FOR THESTONEPOT.PRO
 Domain: thestonepot.pro
========================================

Copy these records and add them to Cloudflare DNS:

──────────────────────────────────────
RECORD 0: Auth Subdomain (Custom Domain for Worker)
──────────────────────────────────────
Type: CNAME
Name: auth
Content: stonepot-oauth.suyesh.workers.dev
TTL: Auto
Proxy status: Proxied (Orange cloud)

NOTE: After adding this DNS record, you need to attach the custom domain
in Cloudflare Dashboard or by deploying with wrangler:
  npx wrangler deploy

──────────────────────────────────────
EMAIL AUTHENTICATION RECORDS
──────────────────────────────────────

──────────────────────────────────────
RECORD 1: SPF (Sender Policy Framework)
──────────────────────────────────────
Type: TXT
Name: @
Content: v=spf1 include:relay.mailchannels.net ~all
TTL: Auto

──────────────────────────────────────
RECORD 2: MailChannels Domain Verification
──────────────────────────────────────
Type: TXT
Name: _mailchannels
Content: v=mc1 cfid=auth.thestonepot.pro
TTL: Auto

──────────────────────────────────────
RECORD 3: DMARC (Email Authentication Policy)
──────────────────────────────────────
Type: TXT
Name: _dmarc
Content: v=DMARC1; p=quarantine; rua=mailto:dmarc@thestonepot.pro; pct=100; adkim=s; aspf=s
TTL: Auto

========================================
 HOW TO ADD IN CLOUDFLARE
========================================

1. Go to: https://dash.cloudflare.com
2. Select domain: thestonepot.pro
3. Click: DNS → Records
4. Click: Add record (for each record above)
5. Fill in Type, Name, and Content
6. Click: Save
7. Repeat for all 3 records
8. Wait 10 minutes for DNS propagation

========================================
 VERIFY DNS RECORDS
========================================

After adding, verify with:
dig TXT thestonepot.pro
dig TXT _mailchannels.thestonepot.pro
dig TXT _dmarc.thestonepot.pro

Or use: https://dnschecker.org
